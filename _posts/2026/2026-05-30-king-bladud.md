Created for the upcoming Blood Bowl tournament called <a href="https://www.feldwick.co/bloodbath26">Bloodbath</a>, this project was commissioned by <a href="https://www.agemaminis.co.uk/">Agema Minis</a> to provide event attendees a unique miniature from the day. Based on the legend of King Bladud, with more than a few creative turns taken to make him a more suitable fit for the fantasy football pitch, and accompanied by his ball boar Mudhog, this was another hugely enjoyable project for me. We were also able to include a very striking alternative version of the miniature which replaces the "boar belly" plate with a gaping, drooling maw!
