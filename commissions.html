If you are looking to have something commissioned, I take on a limited number of projects, working on both individual pieces and larger sets.

Pricing depends on the scope and complexity of the work, and is agreed per project. It is best to contact me first to discuss what you are looking for and I can provide a rough estimate before we work anything out in detail.

I work on both larger commission projects and individual pieces. While I often focus on sets and multi-piece work, I really enjoy individual commissions as well and they’re very welcome.

To enquire, please get in touch via email at callie@renegadegames.comminis.com or through social media messages. You can message me through Mastodon, Bluesky, or Instagram

I look forward to hearing from you.
