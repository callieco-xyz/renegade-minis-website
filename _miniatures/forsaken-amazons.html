Forsaken Amazons are those who have been exiled for their devotion to a god long since rejected by the rest of the pantheon. Forced into the ash-choked volcanic regions deep within the jungle, they persist in isolation, marking their devotion with bone totems and wearing the skulls of scavenging animals that inhabit their lands. While contact with the Forsaken is forbidden among Amazon society, this taboo is often overlooked when it comes to football, a ritual so ingrained in Amazon culture even the exiled are permitted to return to the pitch.

The Forsaken Amazons team is available to purchase right now, you will receive the STL files currently included as soon as you purchase. Over the course of the first 30 days the Founders Pack will be expanded, adding additional miniatures, tokens, and star player options at no extra cost to early buyers. After the campaign period ends, the Founders Pack price will increase to reflect its final contents, although all models included will also remain available individually at standard pricing.

What's in the Forsaken Amazons Founder's Pack

The Founder's Pack contains both pre-supported, and unsupported STL files for the following miniatures. These miniatures are designed to be printed using a resin printer.

The Core Team

  • 6 Forsaken Amazon Linemen, The Vulture Warriors. The backbone of any Forsaken Amazon team, these warriors wear the skulls of great Ash Vultures and bear thick feathered cowls upon their shoulders.
  • 2 Forsaken Amazon Throwers, The Spider Warriors. Dedicated to the great Spider spirits of the Forsaken lands, these throwers are adorned with their sacred symbols, allowing the largest arachnids to nest upon their bodies.
  • 2 Forsaken Amazon Blitzers, The Raptor Warriors. Seasoned hunters of the great cold-blooded lizards of their home, these Blitzers wear raptor skulls as trophies of their skill.
  • 2 Forsaken Amazon Blockers, The Sabretooth Warriors. The Sabretooth is a rare and powerful totem of the jungle; few have seen one, and fewer still have claimed one.
